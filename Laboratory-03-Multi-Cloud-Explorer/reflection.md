# 📝 Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

---

### 🌟 1. Platform That Impressed Me Most
Among the major cloud platforms, **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** impressed me the most due to its clean architectural approach to containerization and artificial intelligence. While AWS dominates market share, GCP's native development of Kubernetes and seamless integration with Vertex AI demonstrate how specialized platforms can excel at cutting-edge tech stacks.

---

### 🤝 2. Core Similarities Across Providers
Despite distinct brand ecosystems, AWS, Azure, and GCP share fundamental architectural paradigms. All three offer equivalent infrastructure primitives:
* **Compute:** On-demand virtual machines (EC2, Azure VMs, Compute Engine)
* **Storage:** Scalable object storage (S3, Blob Storage, Cloud Storage)
* **Identity:** Centralized IAM frameworks
* **Database & Orchestration:** Managed SQL databases and managed Kubernetes environments

---

### 💼 3. Business Requirements & Platform Selection
Choosing a cloud provider is rarely about finding a single "best" platform, but rather identifying the right technical and operational fit. A startup with tight budgets benefits from AWS credits and serverless tooling, while an enterprise heavily reliant on Microsoft 365 minimizes friction by choosing Azure. Platform selection must align directly with budget constraints, existing technical skill sets, legacy infrastructure, and future scaling targets.

### 🎯 4. Key Learnings as a Cloud Solutions Consultant
Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to look beyond brand popularity and evaluate platforms through a pragmatic, objective lens. I learned to systematically analyze real-world client scenarios—balancing cost efficiency, integration complexity, and performance requirements to deliver actionable architectural guidance.
